Three specialisation routes

A specialisation needs 3 courses from its category, including its required ones. Each diagram runs left to right through all 10 buckets. Filled stations on the main line are required. Hollow stations on branches count toward the specialisation, and you choose among them. Buckets with no station are free for any course.

Can you earn two?

On paper, yes: some plans meet the rules for two specialisations at once. The institute's documents don't say whether both would be awarded, so ask before relying on it.

Semester 2 in detail

Two compulsory courses plus two electives. The four buckets below are separate pools: take at most one course from any bucket, two electives in total. Three of these electives are the required starting point of a specialisation.

Artificial & Computational Intelligence

Compulsory for everyone, 5 units. Not an elective and doesn't count toward a specialisation.

Deep Reinforcement Learning

Compulsory for everyone, 4 units. Not an elective and doesn't count toward a specialisation.

Semester 3 in detail

Four electives from six buckets. The documents state the one-per-bucket rule only for Semester 2. This page assumes it applies here too, and the list itself is tentative until the Semester 3 orientation.

Career destinations

24 roles in six groups. Each has three foundation courses (the electives closest to the job), a suggested set of six electives, and evidence from real job postings read in October 2026. Use the highlight switch at the top to see each role's plan under a particular specialisation.
