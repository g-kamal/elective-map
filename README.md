# Elective Map: M.Tech AI/ML (BITS Pilani WILP)

An interactive guide to the Semester 2 and Semester 3 electives, the three specialisations (NLP, Deep Learning, Audio & Vision) and the 24 careers they lead to.

**Live site:** https://g-kamal.github.io/elective-map/

## What's inside

- **The seven lines:** every elective grouped by field, from Semester 2 to Semester 3 to careers.
- **Specialisation routes:** a route map for each specialisation through all 10 buckets, with what's required, what's optional and what each one blocks.
- **Connections map:** which Semester 2 courses build into which Semester 3 courses.
- **Semester 2 and 3 in detail:** every course with topics, tools, maths load, job demand, follow-on courses, content overlaps and career fit.
- **Career destinations:** 24 roles with foundation courses, suggested plans and evidence from 261 job postings (October 2026).
- **Plan checker:** build your six electives and check bucket rules, specialisation progress, units, overlaps and career fit. Plans can be shared as a link.

## How to use

Open the live site in any browser. No sign-up or install needed. Your plan, checklist and theme are saved only in your own browser.

## Data and disclaimer

Built from the course handouts, programme structure slide, Semester 2 elective grouping and the tentative Semester 3 bucket list (October 2026), using data from [Elective Compass](https://github.com/2025ac05759-oss/elective-compass).

Course links, career fits and job-demand ratings are interpretations, not institute rules. The Semester 3 list is tentative. Always confirm your choices against the official list at registration.
